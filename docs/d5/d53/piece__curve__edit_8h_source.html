41/**
42 * Edits made in the piece editor to a curve of a piece. They are stored as attributes of the <node> of the curve, so
43 * the draft does not change and the edits are re-applied to the curve of every size:
44 * simplify="-" or "0.31;0.62" the curve is replaced by a smooth curve with fewer points; the numbers are where the
45 * new points are, as a fraction of the length (so every size gets the same points)
46 * curveOffsets="2:0.5,-0.3" inner point 2 moved by (0.5, -0.3) pattern units
47 * curvePins="2;5" inner points 2 and 5 are pinned: they hold the curve when its ends move
48 * and on the <node> of a point:
49 * bend="true" when this point moves, the curves next to it only move their end with its handle
50 * (instead of following with their whole shape)
51 */

59struct CurveEdit
60{
