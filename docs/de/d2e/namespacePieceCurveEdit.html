Detailed Description

Edits made in the piece editor to a curve of a piece. They are stored as attributes of the <node> of the curve, so the draft does not change and the edits are re-applied to the curve of every size: simplify="-" or "0.31;0.62" the curve is replaced by a smooth curve with fewer points; the numbers are where the new points are, as a fraction of the length (so every size gets the same points) curveOffsets="2:0.5,-0.3" inner point 2 moved by (0.5, -0.3) pattern units curvePins="2;5" inner points 2 and 5 are pinned: they hold the curve when its ends move and on the <node> of a point: bend="true" when this point moves, the curves next to it only move their end with its handle (instead of following with their whole shape)
